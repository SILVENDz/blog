Silver is SEVERYLY underrated

I am a massive Gen 2 lover, and I don't think Silver recieves as much love as he deserves. To be fair, there is only a section of the fandom that actually pays attention to the characters and their development. Regardless, I feel like most of the attention goes to other rivals,such as Blue or N. I am not saying that they don't deserve love, just that Silver deserves more (and I might be slightly biased) His story in HGSS really covers the 'moral' that the game was trying to convey. HGSS and G/S/C pretty much state the importance of the bond between trainers and their pokemon. Not only was it the generation that introduced evolution through friendship, but manifests this message through the rival. Silver started by only caring about the strenght of his pokemon instead of building a bond with them. We see this pretty often through the rival fights, and it is evident that he is pretty stubborn about his views. His character is shaped by his ties with Team Rocket. Even if he hates them, Giovanni is still his father and the reason he resents weakness. As we progress through the game, we see that Silver begins to question himself about his love for his pokemon. We are informed that he and Lance, the champion of the Johto region, had a talk regarding caring for Pokemon. After the player defeats him in the Team Roket Hideaout underneath Mahogny Town, he begins to question if love is really what he needs to properly train his pokemon. After that, we battle him a couple of times, and he seems to be in the bridge between being a total jerk and a nicer trainer. It isn't until you beat the game and challenge the Elite Four on Monday's and Wednesday's that he seems like a completely differernt rival. He shows care for the player's pokemon, making sure to remindf us to heal them properly. It doesn't stop there, once we fight him, we can see that his Golbat evolved into a Crobat. Golbat is one of those pokemon that exclusively evolves through frierndship. If I am not mistaken, it taks a max friendship stat in order for it to evolve. Silver's chracter apports a beautiful story to a game that revolves all around pokemon and their trainers. He is someone that began their journey seeing pokemon as tools, but eventually grew to see them beyond that. I personally believe that his storyline is something rarely spoken about. Silver desrves so much more love, and I will die on that hill.

When we first encounter him in the games, he is looking through the lab's window, seeming planning something. After Professor Elm sends us to retrieve the Togepic egg from Mr. Pokemon, we econter him once again as a passerby. He fights using the starter that has the advantage against the player's starter pokemon. Once we return to Little Root Town, we find out that the starter pokemon was actually stolen by Silver. In just a couple minutes of playthrough, we are able to form the idea that Silver is a jerk. This doesn't get better as the game progresses. He says he wants to become the strongest pokemon trainer, but really has no base for his plan. He treats his pokemon as tools, only focuses on their stremght, and still manages to have severely underleveled pokemon. He is not pushing us as a rival, but rather getting dragged across the floor. It isn't until we fight him in the underground base in Mahogany Town that he begns to consder treating his pokemon correctly. He mentions Lance as the one that spoke to him about the respect he must treat his pokemon with.

Silver is one of those rivals that fits perfectly into their game. He had a beautiful development, and it is still visible through spin-off games like Pokemon Masters. It is such as a shame not that many people care about the Pokemon trainers, they always have an interesting story to tell. Anyways, I really like Silver as a character, if it wasn't noticable.
